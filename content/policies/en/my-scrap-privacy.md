# Privacy Policy (My Scrap)

Individual developer **John.k** operates the on-device scrap app **My Scrap**. This is not a Quartz company product.

- Contact: [ilovejs97@gmail.com](mailto:ilovejs97@gmail.com)

## 1. Information collected
The following may be stored **only on the device**:
- Titles, URLs, notes, folder names
- Favorites, recents, and settings
- Photos the user selects (OCR or save to gallery)

No name, email, phone, location, or account is collected.

## 2. Purposes
Organizing and searching scraps, on-device OCR, web view, local backup/restore.

## 3. Advertising
Google AdMob banner and exit-screen ads may appear. Google may process advertising ID, IP, and ad interactions. See [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Scrap content is not sent to servers for ads.

## 4. Transfers
No central server or paid AI API receives user Content. OCR uses on-device ML Kit. Opening a URL contacts that website.

## 5. Permissions
- Internet: opening saved URLs, ads
- Camera/photos: OCR and gallery save when the user chooses

## 6. Retention
Local SQLite. Uninstall or in-app delete removes data.

## 7. Children
Not limited to a specific age. No knowing collection of children’s personal information.
