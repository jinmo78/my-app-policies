# Privacy Policy (My Scrap)

"My Scrap" (hereinafter the "App") is an on-device app that stores and processes users' scraps and memos **only inside the device**. This Policy explains what information the App handles.

## 1. Information Collected
The App may store the following information **only inside the user's device**.
- Titles, addresses (URLs), memos, and folder names entered and saved by the user
- App usage-related settings such as favorites and recently viewed status
- Photos/captured images selected by the user (when reading text or saving to the gallery)

Account and identity information such as name, email, phone number, and location information is not collected.

## 2. Purpose of Processing
- Organizing and searching scraps, memos, and folders
- On-device OCR (text reading) and saving screen captures to the gallery
- Opening saved addresses in the in-app webview

## 3. Advertising
The App may display banner ads through Google AdMob. In the course of providing ads, Google and advertising partners may collect and process **advertising-related information** such as device advertising ID, IP address, and ad interactions. For details, please refer to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Scrap and memo content entered and saved by the user is not sent to servers for advertising purposes.

## 4. External Transmission
The App does not send user content to a central server or paid AI APIs. Text recognition uses Google ML Kit on the device.

When a user opens a web address, ordinary Internet access to that website occurs. This is for displaying the page selected by the user.

## 5. Permissions
- Internet: Opening saved web addresses
- Camera/photos: Reading text from photos and saving to the gallery (when selected by the user)

## 6. Retention and Deletion
Data is retained only in the device's local storage (such as SQLite). Deleting the App also deletes data stored by the App. Users may also delete items and folders within the App.

## 7. Children
The App is not directed at any specific age group and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
