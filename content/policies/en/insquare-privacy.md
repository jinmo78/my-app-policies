# Privacy Policy (insquare)

insquare (hereinafter the "Service" or "Quartz") carefully protects users' personal information and media assets, and complies with this Privacy Policy pursuant to the Personal Information Protection Act.

## 1. Personal Information and Image Data Collected
insquare collects information necessary to provide an emotional photo archiving and small-community sharing service.
- **Required items for membership registration**: Email address, login password (encrypted), user nickname, profile photo
- **Core media data**: Photo image files uploaded by users after taking them themselves, and metadata included in photos (among Exif information, capture time, camera model settings, etc.; however, GPS location data is automatically removed upon upload or may be controlled by the user to prevent personal information leakage)
- **Automatically collected information**: Device OS version, unique device identifier, access country information, Service usage status logs, and cookies

## 2. Purpose of Collection and Use
- Confirmation of intent to register as a member and prevention of nickname misuse
- Implementation of private photo albums and shared feed diary features within square-format, limited-member rooms (groups)
- Monitoring of large-volume automatic crawling/abuse that excessively occupies server storage and network resources
- Automatic detection and blocking of harmful media and obscene images

## 3. Restrictions on Sharing of Personal Information and Media (Provision to Third Parties)
Photos uploaded to insquare may be viewed only by approved members within "group rooms" that users voluntarily join and create. The Service provider does not arbitrarily disclose such photos externally or transfer/provide them for third-party marketing purposes such as advertising without the user's consent.

## 4. Retention and Destruction Procedures
- **Upon user withdrawal**: The user's profile and account information are deleted immediately and permanently.
- **Special terms for image archives**: Even after a user withdraws, shared photos uploaded by that user may be retained while the room remains, to ensure the normal diary viewing experience of other participants in the shared "group room" the member joined. However, members may delete photos individually before withdrawal, and bulk deletion may also be processed after withdrawal upon request by email (ilovejs97@gmail.com).
