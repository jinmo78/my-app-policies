# Privacy Policy (Woorimal Dalin)

Woorimal Dalin (hereinafter the "Service" or "Quartz") considers users' personal information to be of the utmost importance and complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act and the Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection, etc. This Privacy Policy has been established to inform users how personal information provided when using the Service is collected, used, retained, and destroyed, and what measures are taken to protect personal information.

## 1. Purpose of Collection and Use of Personal Information
Quartz collects and processes the minimum personal information necessary for the following purposes. Personal information being processed will not be used for any purpose other than those stated below, and if the purpose of use changes, Quartz will take necessary measures, such as obtaining separate consent pursuant to Article 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act.
- **Service registration and identity verification**: Confirmation of intent to register as a member; identity verification and authentication for membership services; maintenance and management of membership status; identity verification under the limited identity verification system; prevention of unauthorized use of the Service
- **Personalized quiz service**: Analysis of user learning statistics (accuracy rate, learning pace, weak spelling types) and generation of individual incorrect-answer notes
- **Real-time global leaderboard (ranking) system**: Score verification, registration on overall and regional ranking boards, and real-time display
- **Customer complaints and inquiries**: Receipt of user inconvenience reports, identification of system error causes, and feedback on results

## 2. Personal Information Items Collected and Collection Methods
Quartz collects the following items to provide the Service.
- **Required items at registration**: Email address, password (hashed encryption), nickname
- **Automatically collected items during Service operation**: Access IP address, unique device identifier (UUID/ADID), OS version, browser/app engine information, cookies, abnormal termination logs
- **Gameplay data**: Cleared stage level, game score, per-question correct/incorrect history, attempt time

## 3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information
Users' personal information is destroyed without delay when the purpose of collection and use has been achieved or when the member completes withdrawal. However, where retention is required under applicable laws, Quartz retains member information for the periods prescribed by law as follows.
- **Records of unauthorized Service use**: Retained for 1 year from the withdrawal date to prevent unauthorized use
- **Website visit records (logs) under the Protection of Communications Secrets Act**: Retained for 3 months
- **Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, etc.**:
  - Records related to contracts or withdrawal of offers, etc.: 5 years
  - Records related to payment of prices and supply of goods, etc.: 5 years
  - Records related to consumer complaints or dispute resolution: 3 years

## 4. Procedures and Methods for Destruction of Personal Information
In principle, users' personal information is destroyed without delay once the purpose of collection and use has been achieved. The procedures and methods of destruction are as follows.
- **Destruction procedure**: Information entered by users for membership registration, etc., is stored for a certain period after the purpose has been achieved, in accordance with internal policies and other applicable laws for information protection reasons, and is then destroyed.
- **Destruction method**: Personal information stored in electronic file form is destroyed using technical methods that make the records unreproducible. Personal information printed on paper is destroyed by shredding or incineration.

## 5. Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives, and How to Exercise Them
As data subjects, users may exercise the following personal information protection-related rights at any time.
- Request to access and correct errors in personal information
- Request to delete personal information (may be restricted where other laws designate the information as a collection target)
- Request to suspend processing
These rights may be exercised in writing, by email, or by other means, and Quartz will take action without delay. For children under 14 years of age, the legal representative has the right to access, correct, and delete the child's personal information.

## 6. Installation, Operation, and Refusal of Automatic Personal Information Collection Devices
Quartz may use "cookies" that store and retrieve usage information from time to time to provide personalized services.
- **Purpose of cookies**: To understand the menus and usage patterns of services visited by users and to provide a more convenient UI/UX.
- **How to refuse cookie installation**: Users have the option to accept or refuse cookies. By configuring web browser options, users may allow all cookies, confirm each time a cookie is stored, or refuse storage of all cookies.
  *(Example: For Chrome, go to [Settings] > [Privacy and security] > [Clear browsing data and cookie settings])*

## 7. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information
Quartz takes the following technical, administrative, and physical measures to ensure the security of personal information.
- **Password encryption**: Members' passwords are stored using a one-way hash algorithm, so even administrators cannot identify them.
- **Technical measures against hacking, etc.**: Encrypted communications (SSL) are used to prevent interception during transmission, and an intrusion prevention system is used to defend against external network attacks.
- **Minimization and training of personnel handling personal information**: Employees who handle personal information are designated, assigned passwords, and granted highly restricted access.
