# Privacy Policy (Korean Master)

Individual developer **John.k** operates **Korean Master (우리말 달인)**. This is not a Quartz company product.

- Privacy officer: John.k
- Contact: [ilovejs97@gmail.com](mailto:ilovejs97@gmail.com)

## 1. Information collected
- **Nickname**: display and rankings; a guest name if none is entered
- **Play records**: scores, history, per-mode rankings
- **Anonymous ID**: Firebase anonymous auth UID, to keep a record on the device
- **Google account (optional)**: email, name, and profile photo only if the user links Google

No legal name, address, phone, or payment data. No ad SDK or ad tracking.

No app-specific password. Email is collected only if Google is linked.

## 2. Purposes
Running quizzes, rankings and history, restoring a record after a device change when Google is linked.

## 3. Processors
Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

## 4. Retention and deletion
Kept while the Service is used. Users may unlink Google, or delete profile, history, and ranking via the app or email.

## 5. Children
No knowing collection from children under 14. Such data is deleted if found.
