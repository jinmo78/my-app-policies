# Privacy Policy (STAIR KING)

Individual developer **John.k** operates the stair climbing game **STAIR KING**. STAIR KING **collects no personal information**. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics.

- Contact: [ilovejs97@gmail.com](mailto:ilovejs97@gmail.com)

## 1. Information collected
None. There is no sign-up or sign-in, and nothing is sent to the developer.

## 2. Information stored on the device
Game progress is kept **only on this device**:

- Stars and best records for each stage
- Coins and unlocked characters
- Sound settings

None of it is sent to anyone, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

## 3. Network use
**1 VS 1** connects two devices on the same Wi-Fi directly to each other.

- What is exchanged: the room name, the number used to generate the stairs (the seed), and how many steps the opponent has climbed
- Nothing passes through the developer's servers or any other server, and nothing leaves the local network.
- Without this mode, the app makes no network connections at all.

## 4. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|------|------|
| `INTERNET` | Connecting directly to the other device on the same Wi-Fi |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` / `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE` | Checking whether Wi-Fi is connected |
| `CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE` | Finding rooms on the same Wi-Fi (UDP broadcast) |

They are not used for anything else.

## 5. Third parties and processors
None. No ad SDK, analytics SDK, or crash reporting tool is included. Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to share with or hand to a third party.

## 6. Children
Because no personal information is collected, nothing is collected from users under 14 either.

## 7. Changes
If the policy changes, this document is updated and the last-updated date is revised.
