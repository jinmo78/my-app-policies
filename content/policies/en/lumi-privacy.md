# Privacy Policy (Lumi)

Individual developer **John.k** operates the AI character chat app **Lumi**. This is not a Quartz company product.

- Privacy officer: John.k
- Contact and deletion: [ilovejs97@gmail.com](mailto:ilovejs97@gmail.com)

## 1. Information collected
- **Account**: anonymous UID; email and password (hashed by Supabase) if registered; nickname and gender if set; Kakao, Google, or Apple identifiers
- **Content**: character profiles and images, chat messages, memories and episode progress, comments
- **Wallet**: Lights (ad rewards) and usage limits
- **Push**: FCM token and platform
- **Ads**: AdMob may process advertising ID, IP, approximate device data, and ad interactions
- **Technical**: minimal app/OS data for troubleshooting

## 2. Purposes
Sign-in, AI chat and image generation, limits and ads, push, abuse prevention, support.

## 3. Processors
| Party | Role |
|------|------|
| Supabase | Auth, database, image storage |
| OpenAI | Chat, suggestions, images, embeddings |
| Render | API hosting |
| Firebase | FCM push only |
| Kakao, Google, Apple | Social sign-in |
| Google AdMob | Ads |

Chat and character text/images may be sent to these processors for the purposes above. Personal information is not sold.

## 4. Advertising
Banner, interstitial, rewarded, and exit ads may appear. See [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Core features work if iOS tracking is denied.

## 5. Retention and deletion
Account and Content are kept until a deletion request or service end. Some messages and characters can be deleted in the app. Email to delete an entire account.

## 6. Children
Not directed at children under 13. No knowing collection of children’s data.
