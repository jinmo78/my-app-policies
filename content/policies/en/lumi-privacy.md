# Privacy Policy (Lumi)

**John.k** operates the AI character chat app **Lumi**.

- Privacy officer: John.k
- Contact and deletion: [ilovejs97@gmail.com](mailto:ilovejs97@gmail.com)

## 1. Information collected
- **Account**: a temporary browse ID; email and password (stored encrypted) if registered; nickname and gender if set; Kakao, Google, or Apple sign-in info
- **Content**: character profiles and photos, chat, memories and episode progress, comments
- **Usage limits**: Lights (continue by watching ads) and daily chat amounts
- **Alerts**: device info needed to send notifications
- **Ads**: Google ads may process an advertising identifier, network address, approximate device data, and ad taps or views
- **Technical**: minimal app and phone type data for troubleshooting

## 2. Purposes
Sign-in, AI chat and image creation, usage limits and ads, alerts, abuse prevention, support.

## 3. Processors
| Role | Description |
|------|------|
| Accounts, chats, images | Cloud servers |
| Chat and image generation | External AI |
| Alerts | Cloud notifications |
| Sign-in | Kakao, Google, Apple |
| Ads | Google ads |

Chat and character text or photos may go through those processes for the purposes above. Personal information is not sold.

## 4. Advertising
Ads may appear at the bottom of the screen, in the middle of use, after watching to extend a limit, or when leaving. The app still works if tracking is denied on iPhone. See [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Retention and deletion
Account and Content are kept until a deletion request or service end. Some messages and characters can be deleted in the app. Email to delete an entire account.

## 6. Children
Not directed at children under 13. No knowing collection of children’s data.
