# Privacy Policy (Lumi)

"Lumi" (Lumi, hereinafter the "App" or "Service") is a mobile application that allows users to converse with AI characters. This Policy explains what information is collected, used, and retained when using the Service, and why.

Operator: Quartz  
Contact: [ilovejs97@gmail.com](mailto:ilovejs97@gmail.com)

## 1. Information Collected

### (1) Account information
- Upon anonymous login: User identifier (UID) issued by the Service
- Upon email registration/login: Email address, information necessary for authentication, and (optional) nickname

### (2) Content entered or created by users
- Character information (name, greeting, persona, appearance description, profile image)
- Conversation messages (user messages and AI responses)
- In-app usage records such as recent conversation lists (may be stored locally on the device)

### (3) Automatically collected / technical information
- Ordinary logs for Service provision, security, and error response (access time, approximate device/app version, etc.)
- If paid features or in-app purchases are added in the future, information necessary for payment processing (this Policy will be revised and announced at that time)

## 2. Purpose of Use
- Member identification and login
- Character creation and management, and provision of AI conversations
- AI appearance recommendations and profile image generation
- Ensuring Service stability, preventing unauthorized use, and responding to customer inquiries
- (Future) Provision of paid features, subscriptions, and in-app purchases

## 3. Processing Entrustment and External Services
The following external services may be used to operate the Service.
- **Supabase**: Account authentication, database, and file (image) storage
- **OpenAI**: Conversation response generation, and image/text recommendation generation
- **Cloud hosting (e.g., Render)**: API server operation

Conversation and character-related text/images entered by users may be transmitted to these services to achieve the purposes above. Personal information is not sold except as required by law.

## 4. Advertising
The App currently does not use an in-app advertising SDK. If introduced, this Policy will be revised and announced.

## 5. Retention Period
- Accounts and Content: Until membership withdrawal or deletion request (or until Service termination)
- Where applicable laws impose separate retention obligations, information may be retained for the relevant period.

For deletion or access requests, please contact [ilovejs97@gmail.com](mailto:ilovejs97@gmail.com). Requests will be processed within a reasonable period.

## 6. Users' Rights
Users may request confirmation or correction of account information, deletion of Content, logout from email accounts, and discontinuation of Service use.  
The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Security
We strive to protect information through reasonable industry measures (such as HTTPS communication). However, complete security cannot be guaranteed during Internet transmission and storage.

## 8. Changes to the Policy
If the contents change, the revised Policy will be posted on this page together with the revision date.
