# Privacy Policy (Split Bill Calculator)

Individual developer **John.k** operates **Split Bill Calculator**. This is not a Quartz company product.

- Privacy officer: John.k

## 1. Information collected
This app is **not local-only**. After sign-in, the following is stored on a server:
- **Account**: email, password (hashed by Supabase)
- **Profile**: display name, session id (single-device sign-in)
- **Settlements**: gatherings, members, expense items and amounts, invite emails and codes
- **Bank accounts**: alias, holder, account number if the user enters them
- **Cookies**: session

No ad SDK.

## 2. Purposes
Sign-in, splitting costs, invites, attaching a saved account, single-device sessions, support.

## 3. Processors
Supabase (auth and database). Settlement and account details are not used for advertising.

## 4. Retention and deletion
Kept while the account is used. Individual accounts and gatherings can be deleted in the app. Email to delete the entire user account.

## 5. Children
Bank details of children under 14 are not collected knowingly. Use by ages 14 and up is recommended.

## 6. Security
HTTPS is used. Account numbers can be sensitive; avoid shared devices.
