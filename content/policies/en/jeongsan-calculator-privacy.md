# Privacy Policy (Jeongsan Calculator)

Jeongsan Calculator (hereinafter the "Service" or "Quartz") does its best to protect users' personal information and rights, and declares this Privacy Policy pursuant to the Personal Information Protection Act.

## 1. Special Nature of Personal Information Collection Methods (Important)
This Jeongsan Calculator Service is, by default, a **locally running application** in which all calculations take place inside the user's device (browser) without server transmission.
- **Local browser storage information**: Names of participants, expense item names, amounts, and other information entered by users for settlement are stored solely in the user's device browser storage (LocalStorage). This information is never transmitted to the Company's servers, and the Company has neither the authority nor the technical means to access such settlement data.
- **Required items collected upon membership registration/login**: The following information is collected only when a user voluntarily creates an account to use cloud sync and backup features.
  - Required items: Email address, password (encrypted), user nickname
- **Automatically collected items**: Anonymized crash logs for Service stability, device OS type, screen resolution, Service visit logs, and cookies

## 2. Purpose of Collection and Use
- **Backup account holders**: Provide recovery and synchronization of existing settlement groups and detailed records when changing devices or clearing cache
- **General users**: Analyze Service usage statistics, fix bugs, and optimize the system
- **Customer support**: Handle user complaints and transmit feedback

## 3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information
If a user clears the device cache or deletes settlement data in LocalStorage, the information is destroyed immediately and permanently. Information of users who created sync accounts is permanently deleted from the server immediately upon withdrawal. However, where transaction evidence obligations arise under applicable laws (e.g., the Electronic Commerce Act), the information may be securely retained for the required period (up to 5 years).

## 4. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information
Although Quartz operates primarily on a local basis, it applies the following measures against security vulnerabilities that may arise during cloud sync and local storage.
- SSL/HTTPS encryption applied to all sync transmission segments
- In-browser encryption schema to prevent leakage upon device theft
- Passwords protected with one-way hash encryption (SHA-256 or higher) that cannot be decrypted bidirectionally
