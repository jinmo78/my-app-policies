## 1. Information collected
This app does **not** keep data only on this device. After sign-in, the following is stored in the cloud:
- **Account**: email, password (stored encrypted)
- **Profile**: display name, a value so only one device stays signed in
- **Settlements**: gatherings, members, expense items and amounts, invite emails and codes
- **Bank accounts**: alias, holder, account number if the user enters them
- **Sign-in stay**: login info the browser remembers for a while

No ads.

## 2. Purposes
Sign-in, splitting costs, invites, attaching a saved account, single-device sign-in, support.

## 3. Processors
Accounts and settlement data are entrusted to cloud servers. Settlement and account details are not used for advertising.

## 4. Retention and deletion
Kept while the account is used. Individual accounts and gatherings can be deleted in the app. Email to delete the entire user account.

## 5. Children
Bank details of children under 14 are not collected knowingly. Use by ages 14 and up is recommended.

## 6. Security
Connections are encrypted. Account numbers can be sensitive; avoid shared devices.
