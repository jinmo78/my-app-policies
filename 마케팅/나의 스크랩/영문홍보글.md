## 예전 재게시안 (참고 — 스토어 언급 포함, 필터에 걸릴 수 있음)

### Title

Built a simple “save for later” app because my chat history became a graveyard of unread links

### Body

Hi everyone,

I’m a solo developer. I built a small app called **My Scrap** to fix a problem I kept running into myself.

**The problem**

I constantly find good articles, tips, and resources while browsing. My old habit was dumping links into my own chat room or browser bookmarks. Later I’d:
- forget where I saved them
- lose them in a wall of messages
- never open the bookmarks again

Existing scrap / read-later tools felt too heavy or subscription-heavy for what I needed.

**What I built**

A lightweight place to collect links with:
- one-tap save via the native Share sheet
- categories + tags
- fast search when I actually need something
- a clean UI focused only on saving and finding links

It’s available on iOS and Android. Happy to share store links in the comments if anyone wants to try it.

I’d love feedback on the UX, missing features, or anything that feels unnecessary. Thanks for reading!

## 예전 게시용 본문 예시 (참고 — 스토어 링크 포함, 필터에 걸릴 수 있음)

Hi everyone! 👋

I’m a solo developer and I just released a project I’ve been working on: **My Scrap** (나의 스크랩).

### 💡 Why I built this
Like many of you, I constantly stumble upon great articles, blog posts, and resources while browsing the web.

My old workflow was a mess:
- Pasting links into my own messaging app (which got buried instantly)
- Saving browser bookmarks that I'd never look at again
- Using existing tools that felt overly complex or required pricey subscriptions

I just wanted a **simple, fast, and clutter-free place** to collect and organize links I actually care about. So I decided to build it myself during my free time.

---

### ✨ Key Features
- **One-Tap Saving:** Easily save links via the native Share sheet from any browser or app.
- **Smart Organization:** Categorize and tag your saved content so you can retrieve it in seconds.
- **Search & Retrieval:** Fast full-text or tag-based search.
- **Clean & Focused UI:** No bloatware or confusing dashboards—just your collected resources.
